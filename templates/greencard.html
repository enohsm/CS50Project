Green Card Insurance

Green Card insurance is an important matter for travelers planning to go abroad with their vehicles — and not just frequent travelers, but anyone who intends to take their car outside the country. Green Card insurance provides international coverage for your vehicle, making it a crucial service for trips abroad.

With the Green Card insurance request system, this process can be handled quite easily. So how does the system work?

  • First, the customer registers a vehicle in the system.
  • Then, they create a Green Card insurance request.
  • The service provider begins preparing the insurance.
  • Once the insurance is ready, the service provider updates the “Request Status” accordingly.
  • When the insurance is completed, the customer receives their Green Card insurance.
